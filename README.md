# 🌾 1990s Gram-Bangla — Kalbaishakhi & Monsoon Edition

An interactive, nostalgic audiovisual simulation of a 1990s rural Bangladeshi village (*Gram-Bangla*), built entirely with native **HTML5 Canvas 2D** and procedural **Web Audio API** in a single standalone file.

Experience the peaceful countryside transition through the day (*Prohor*), or summon the fury of a sudden seasonal summer tempest (*Kalbaishakhi Jhor*).

---

## ✨ Features

### 🌧️ Dynamic Weather & Rain Engine (*Brishti*)
- **Real-Time Intensity Scrubbing**: Smooth slider transitions from **Shanto (Calm)**, to **Rimjhim Brishti (Light Drizzle)**, **Barsha (Monsoon Rain)**, up to a full **Kalbaishakhi Jhor (Tempest)**.
- **Realistic Wind & Slant**: Raindrops dynamically adjust their angle and velocity with gale-force winds.
- **Physical Tin-Roof Impacts**: Slanted raindrops collide with curved corrugated tin roofs (*Chouchala*) and thatched kitchens (*Rannaghar*), spawning reactive water splashes and ground ripples.
- **Dynamic Lightning (*Biddut*)**: Procedural forked lightning generation with sky illumination and river reflections.

### 🔊 Procedural Web Audio Synthesizer
- **No External Audio Files**: 100% synthesized procedural sound effects using pure Web Audio API oscillators, biquad filters, and pink/white noise buffers.
- **Tin-Roof Rain Resonance**: Authentic high-frequency metallic pattering sound of heavy rain beating on corrugated sheet tin.
- **Howling Wind Gusts**: Real-time modulated bandpass wind howling with pitch shifts.
- **Rolling Plain Thunder**: Low-frequency resonant rumbles with acoustic delays simulating distance across the river.

### 🌅 Day–Night Cycle (*Prohor*)
- **6 Keyframe Atmospheres**:
  - 🌌 *Nijhum Raat* (Midnight)
  - 🌅 *Bhor* (Dawn)
  - ☀️ *Dupur* (Midday)
  - 🌇 *Godhuli* (Golden Dusk)
  - 🌆 *Sondhya Raat* (Evening Twilight)
- Manual time-scrubbing slider and auto-cycle toggle.

### 🏡 Authentic 1990s Gram-Bangla Elements
- **Chouchala Tin-and-Mud House**: Authentic curved roof architecture on a raised mud plinth (*Pota*).
- **Hanging Hurricane Lantern (*Hariken*)**: Flickering warm glow on the veranda with wet courtyard reflections during downpours.
- **Country Boat (*Nouka*)**: Pitches and bobs dynamically on river waves with its own canopy lantern.
- **Wildlife & Flora**:
  - *Shapla* (National water lilies) floating in river shallows.
  - Flocks of birds heading home faster to take shelter when rain starts.
  - *Jonaki Poka* (Fireflies) glowing softly on calm dark nights.
  - Golden paddy fields (*Dhan Khet*) and a bamboo jetty (*Ghat*).
